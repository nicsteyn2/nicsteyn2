## Hi there 👋

I am a recent doctoral graduate from the [Modern Statistics and Statistical Machine Learning CDT](https://statml.io) at the University of Oxford, supervised by Professor Christl Donnelly and Dr Kris V Parag.

Currently, I am working as a research consultant at [Verdel Instruments](https://verdelinstruments.com), where I am developing statistical and machine learning methods to analyse 2D mass spectrometry data. **I am open to other opportunities in the science start-up space. Please reach out if you have an interesting problem that might benefit from my skills.**

My [full doctoral thesis can be found here](https://ora.ox.ac.uk/objects/uuid:e045db75-76e4-440c-9103-30f24eac754b). Key pieces of work from my thesis include:

- A decision-theoretic framework for uncertainty quantification in epidemiological models ([publication](https://doi.org/10.1093/aje/kwag154)/[GitHub repo](https://github.com/nicsteyn2/DecisionTheoreticEpi))
 - Robust uncertainty quantification in popular epidemic models ([publication](https://doi.org/10.1093/aje/kwaf165)/[GitHub repo](https://github.com/nicsteyn2/RobustRtEstimators)/[vignettes](https://nicsteyn2.github.io/RobustRtEstimators/))
 - SMC methods for epidemic renewal models ([publication](https://doi.org/10.1002/sim.70204)/[GitHub repo](https://github.com/nicsteyn2/SMCforRt)/[website](https://nicsteyn2.github.io/SMCforRt/))
 - Smoothing methods for epidemic survey data ([publication](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1013515)/[GitHub repo](https://github.com/nicsteyn2/EpidemicSurveySmoothing/tree/main)/[vignettes](https://github.com/nicsteyn2/EpidemicSurveySmoothing/tree/main/examples))
 - Estimating epidemiological dynamics using wastewater data ([publication](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43856-024-00570-3)/[GitHub repo](https://github.com/nicsteyn2/NZWastewaterModelling))
- A descriptive analysis of behaviours during the COVID-19 pandemic in England ([publication](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjph-2025-002851)/[data portal](https://m-whit-ic.shinyapps.io/react-social-shiny/)).

During my doctoral studies I also worked on [estimating orphanhood due to COVID-19 and all-causes in Brazil](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lana.2025.101252) and further [estimation of the reproduction number](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s44263-026-00280-z) ([see also](https://royalsocietypublishing.org/rsta/article/383/2293/20240412/234655/Simulation-based-inference-of-the-time-dependent)).
